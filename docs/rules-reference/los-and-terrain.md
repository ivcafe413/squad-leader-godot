# Rules Reference — Line of Sight & Terrain

Scope: LOS and terrain rules needed for the Scenario 1 board only.

## Summary
- LOS between firing unit's hex and target hex is traced hex-to-hex; certain terrain (woods, buildings, hills) blocks or degrades LOS depending on elevation and intervening terrain.
- Each terrain type present on the Scenario 1 board has: a movement cost, a Terrain Effects Modifier (TEM) applied to fire combat against units in that terrain, and LOS-blocking behavior.
