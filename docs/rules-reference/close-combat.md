# Rules Reference — Close Combat

Scope: close combat rules needed for Scenario 1 only.

## Summary (rulebook §20)
- Close Combat occurs only during the Close Combat Phase, between opposing units in the same hex; unlike Fire Combat it is considered **simultaneous** — both sides fire on each other, even if one or both sides are eliminated (§20.1–20.2).
- **Odds calculation (§20.3):** total firepower of attacking units vs. total firepower of defending units, expressed as a ratio (e.g. two 6-2-8 squads vs. one 4-6-7 squad = 12-4 = 3-1). Fractions round down in favor of the defender.
- The Close Combat Table (Quick Reference Data Card) is cross-indexed by these odds to get a Kill Number; roll 2 dice — a result ≤ the Kill Number eliminates the attacked unit(s); reroll separately for each distinct Close Combat attack (§20.3–20.4).
- The attacker may split their attacks across multiple unit combinations in any hex, as long as every unit attacks at most once, and must designate all such attacks before rolling any dice (§20.4).
- Leadership modifiers from one predesignated leader in the hex may apply to one Close Combat dice roll per player turn (§20.6).
- Units that survive combat but remain in the hex with no side eliminated are **locked in Melee** and may not leave, or take any other action, until one side is eliminated (§20.8, 20.81–20.84).
- **Broken units play no role in Close Combat** — if a broken unit ends up alone in a Close Combat hex, it is eliminated (§20.9).
- LMGs may add their firepower to an attacking unit's Close Combat firepower but are not counted for a defending unit's firepower, and MGs/LMGs have no penetration factor in Close Combat (§20.5).
