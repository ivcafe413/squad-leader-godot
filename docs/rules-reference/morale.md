# Rules Reference — Morale

Scope: morale rules needed for Scenario 1 only.

## Summary (rulebook §12–15)
- **Morale Check (§12.1–12.2):** roll 2 dice; passes if ≤ the unit's printed morale rating. Triggered only by an `M`/`(#)` fire result or loss of a leader in the same hex (§12.2, 12.22).
- Units that fail are **broken**: inverted, and removed from the game if they fail again while already broken (§12.3, §13.6).
- **Broken units (§13):** may not fire or engage in Close Combat (§13.1); must move away from an adjacent enemy unit in the next Rout Phase (§13.2); automatically seek cover in woods/buildings and rout at maximum speed toward the nearest cover if not already there or adjacent to an enemy (§13.4); abandon all carried support weapons while routing (§13.5). Units may voluntarily break at any time (§13.7).
- **Rally (§14):** during the Rally Phase, a broken squad rolls ≤ its morale number (2 dice) to rally, modified by an unbroken friendly leader's leadership rating in the same hex (§14.1–14.2). A broken leader may attempt to self-rally (§14.3). No penalty for a failed rally attempt (§14.7). Rolling an unmodified 12 on a rally attempt eliminates the unit and forces all other friendly units in the hex to take a normal Morale Check (§18.3).
- **Desperation Morale (§14.6):** a unit that has been fired on (with effect) since the last Rally Phase must rally at 4 less than its normal morale (harder to rally) unless it's American — American squads always rally at normal morale plus leadership modifiers (§50.1).
- **Berserk (§18.4, Russian units only):** rolling an unmodified "2" on a Morale Check makes the unit immune to further Morale Checks but forces it to charge the nearest enemy unit each turn.

### Leadership (§15)
- A leader's leadership rating (usually negative, i.e. beneficial) modifies the dice roll of one Fire Group per player turn (if the leader fires with it) and Morale Checks/Rally attempts for all units in the same hex (excluding the leader himself for Morale Checks, §15.6).
- Modifiers from two leaders in the same hex are not cumulative; only the better one applies (§15.7).
- A leader who breaks or is eliminated immediately stops providing any benefit (§15.5).
