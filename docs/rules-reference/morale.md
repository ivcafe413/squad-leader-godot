# Rules Reference — Morale

Scope: morale rules needed for Scenario 1 only.

## Summary
- Fire combat results (and other triggers) can require a unit to take a morale check against its printed morale level.
- Failing produces a status change (e.g. pinned, broken) which restricts the unit's future actions (fire, movement) until rallied.
- Leaders may modify morale checks and assist rally attempts in the Rally Phase.
