# Rules Reference — Movement

Scope: movement rules needed for Scenario 1 only.

## Summary (rulebook §5–6, 19)
Scenario 1 ("The Guards Counterattack") is played entirely on **mapboard 1** (the city board), Game Set I (§3.1). No hills, walls, or hedges appear on board 1 (§11.5), so only the base terrain costs below apply.

- Movement Allowance: every squad has 4 MF, every leader has 6 MF (§5.41–5.42). Support weapons have no MF of their own and must be carried (§5.43).
- A unit spends MF from its own turn's allowance only; MF cannot be transferred between units or accumulated across turns (§5.2–5.3).
- **MF cost table (§5.5):**

  | Terrain | Cost |
  |---|---|
  | Open Ground, Shellhole, Wheatfield | 1 MF |
  | Onto Road from a non-road hexside | 1 MF |
  | Onto Road from a road hexside | 1/2 MF |
  | Woods | 2 MF |
  | Enter any Building | 2 MF |
  | Move within Building | 2 MF |
  | Over Walls or Hedges | 1 MF + cost of terrain moved into (not applicable on board 1, §11.5) |

- Terrain effects/costs are cumulative for hexes containing more than one terrain feature (§5.54).
- Leader movement bonus: a squad that spends its *entire* Movement Phase stacked with a leader gets +2 MF (§5.44).
- Portage: squads may carry up to 3 portage points of support weapons at no MF cost (up to 5 during the Advance Phase, §5.73); leaders up to 3 (Advance Phase) / lose 1 MF per portage point over their free capacity otherwise (§5.7, 5.71–5.76).
- All infantry units are moved one at a time; a moving player may not backtrack a unit once it stops (§19.1–19.4).
- No "free minimum move" rule exists in these base rules — a unit simply cannot enter a hex it can't afford (§5.1–5.2, §19.1).

## Stacking (§6)
- Max 4 infantry units per hex, of which at most 3 may be squads, plus up to 10 portage points of support weapons (§6.1).
- Stacking limits may be temporarily exceeded during the Movement Phase as long as hexes aren't overstacked by phase end (§6.3).
